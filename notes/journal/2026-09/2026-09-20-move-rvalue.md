# `move` becomes a mirlite rvalue; the Miri-mismatch prediction was wrong

[OBS 2026-09-20] Ran real Miri (toolchain nightly-2026-06-01 installed
today, miri 0.1.0 14210df0e2) on `let mut s = S(5); let p = &raw mut
s.0; consume(s); unsafe { *p }`: OK, prints 5. Every variant OK. The
emitted MIR explains it: `_4 = move _1; _3 = consume(move _4)` — a
named local moved into a call goes through a temporary, and the
assignment move is a copy. Miri's in-place protection hits `_4`. So
yesterday's claim that such a program was a latent pass-vs-Miri mismatch
is wrong; superseded in move-deinits-its-source-at-calls.md. Miri's own
`arg_inplace_observe_after` uses custom MIR, and DOES fail
("uninitialized") on this toolchain — in-place passing is on.

[FACT 2026-09-20, verified against af0b1d7+] `RExpr.move src` added:
semantics mint/read/die (see move-is-a-temporary-unique-reborrow.md),
