## The week's semantic facts worth remembering

Raw-mut retags insert above the granting item with NO access (that one
placement rule yields SRW sibling coexistence). Reads DISABLE Uniques
in place — removal would merge SRW groups. Writes through SRW stay
within the contiguous SRW run. Protection is weak on SRW items.
Fn-entry retags cover tuple fields but NOT named-struct fields, and
Box gets a (weakly) protected Unique retag. Int-to-ptr needs no
angelic choice here: concrete bump-allocator addresses make
address→allocation a function; provenance lives in the SB state, never
in integers. Shims that replace calls must reproduce the callee's
fn-entry retag.
